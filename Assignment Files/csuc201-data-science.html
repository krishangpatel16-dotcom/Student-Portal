Fundamentals of Data Science

Questions

  1. Define data science and explain how it differs from data analytics and machine learning.
  2. For the values 4, 5, 5, 7, and 9, calculate the mean, median, and mode.
  3. Explain the difference between a population and a sample. Give an example from student survey data.
  4. Describe four steps used to clean a dataset containing missing values, duplicate rows, and inconsistent date formats.
  5. What is the difference between a categorical variable and a numerical variable? Give two examples of each.
  6. Explain correlation and why correlation alone does not establish causation.
  7. Describe how a train/test split helps evaluate a predictive model and identify a common source of data leakage.
  8. For a dataset with a strongly skewed distribution and outliers, compare the usefulness of the mean and median.
  9. Explain precision, recall, and accuracy using the context of a classifier that flags at-risk students.
  10. Propose a clear chart for comparing enrollment across five courses and explain your choice.
