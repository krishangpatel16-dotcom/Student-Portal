Questions

  1. Construct the truth table for $(p \rightarrow q) \land (\neg q \rightarrow \neg p)$ and state whether the expression is a tautology.
  2. Use a truth table to determine whether $((p \lor q) \land \neg p) \rightarrow q$ is logically valid.
  3. Prove by mathematical induction that $1+3+5+\cdots +(2n-1)=n^2$ for every positive integer $n$.
  4. Let $A=\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and define $aRb$ when $a-b$ is divisible by 2. Determine whether $R$ is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive.
  5. For $f:\mathbb{Z}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}$ defined by $f(x)=3x+2$, determine whether $f$ is injective and whether it is surjective.
  6. A group has 8 students. How many different 3-student committees can be formed? Explain the counting method.
  7. How many distinct arrangements can be made from the letters in the word LEVEL?
  8. Draw a graph with degree sequence $(3,2,2,1)$, or explain why no such simple graph exists. Verify your conclusion using the handshaking lemma.
  9. Find the shortest path from A to D in a weighted graph with edges AB=4, AC=2, BC=1, BD=5, and CD=8. Show the candidate path lengths.
  10. Use De Morgan's laws to simplify $\neg((p \land q) \lor r)$ and verify the result with a truth table.
