ABOUT THE PORTAL

Everything You Need for Your Academic Life

The Student Portal is a centralized digital platform created to provide students with quick and convenient access to their academic information.

Students can manage important academic activities such as courses, assignments, attendance, timetable, results and feedback from one place.
